Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Morgan Studio / Streaming Foundations

This Privacy Policy describes how Streaming Income Foundations and Morgan Studio ("we", "us", or "our") collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website, enroll in our courses, or contact our support team.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Contact Details: Your name and email address when you register for training or contact customer support.
  • Transaction Data: Details of transactions completed via our third-party payment processors (such as Stripe). We do not store or process complete credit card numbers directly on our servers.
  • Technical Information: Device type, browser characteristics, IP address, and standard log data collected automatically to ensure website security and proper operation.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected strictly for legitimate business purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and deliver our online educational materials and platform access.
  • To process transactions and provide transaction confirmations and invoices.
  • To respond to your inquiries, customer support requests, and technical assistance needs.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical or security issues and prevent fraudulent activity.

3. Data Protection and Security

We implement standard technical and organizational security measures designed to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, destruction, loss, alteration, disclosure, or misuse. All payment data is encrypted using 256-bit TLS encryption and handled directly by PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share data with essential third-party service providers who assist us in operating our platform (e.g., cloud hosting, payment gateways, and email dispatchers) under strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate data, or request deletion of your information. To exercise any of these rights, please contact our privacy desk.
